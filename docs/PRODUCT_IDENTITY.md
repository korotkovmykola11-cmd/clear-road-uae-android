## Who is Clear Road?

**Clear Road is a calm route decision assistant for UAE drivers.**

Not a navigator. Not a map. Not a chatbot.

Clear Road appears at the **moment of decision** — when the driver needs to understand *which route to take and why*, before opening Google Maps or Waze.

**Core formula:**

```
Clear Road decides
→ User understands
→ Google Maps / Waze navigate
```

**Primary value:** clarity of choice in 2–3 seconds.  
**Not value:** ETA arms race, fuel analytics, route encyclopedia.

## Style

**Calm Decision Interface** — a decision surface, not a navigation surface.

| Yes | No |
|-----|-----|
| Air and spacing | Dashboard overload |
| Short blocks | Long paragraphs |
| Calm typography | Metric obsession |
| Soft contrast | Aggressive CTA |
| UAE premium calm | Neon startup chaos |
| Decision-first hierarchy | Map-first hierarchy |

### Sacred blocks (product face)

1. **Choice** — what to pick  
2. **Why** — why (short)  
3. **Tip** — what to expect  

Any redesign may change wrapping. It must not change semantics.

### Simplified Why Layer (brand language)

Explanation style = **tags, not essays:**

- No Salik
- 8 min faster
- Fewer turns
- Main roads

## Premium or friendly?

**Premium Calm — human warmth without playful mascot energy.**

Not cold luxury. Not childish friendly.

| Scale | Position |
|-------|----------|
| Premium ↔ Budget | Premium-leaning (calm quality) |
| Formal ↔ Casual | Calm casual |
| Serious ↔ Playful | ~80% serious / ~20% warm |
| Expert ↔ Friend | Experienced friend |

Fuel cost is **not** part of brand expression in the UI. Driver research confirmed: **time and Salik matter most.**

## Hero

**The hero is the driver making a clear choice — not YUNO, not the app.**

Clear Road is not the protagonist. Clear Road helps the driver choose with confidence.

**Hero moment:**

> Driver reads Choice / Why / Tip → understands → opens Maps or Waze with confidence.

**Brand line:**

> *Clear Road doesn't drive for you. It helps you choose with confidence.*

## Visual signature: Clear Sky Blue

**Locked in Stage 21.5** — premium light theme, white surfaces, soft clouds, Dubai atmosphere.

**Dubai clear-sky blue is a brand accent — atmosphere, not a travel poster.**

| Yes | No |
|-----|-----|
| Sky blue as clarity accent | Burj Khalifa wallpaper on every in-app screen |
| Soft cloud atmosphere | Tourist UAE postcard aesthetic |
| White surfaces, minimal shadows | Map-first blue navigation UI |
| Open road / visibility metaphor | Dark dashboard overload |
| Warm sand / stone as secondary | Neon startup chaos |

**Metaphor:** *Clear sky above · Clear decision below*

Suggested palette roles:

- **Clear Sky Blue** — primary accent, decision highlight
- **Warm Sand / Stone** — calm surfaces
- **Deep Road Grey** — stable text
- **Neutral tags** — time and Salik labels without alarm red
